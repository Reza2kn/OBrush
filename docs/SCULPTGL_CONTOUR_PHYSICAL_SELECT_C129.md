# C129: physical source multi-selection on closed parts

The C128 open-quad fixture could be selected through SculptGL's application method, but an attempted physical Shift-click missed it. A new independently authored SGL v3 fixture contains two **closed six-face boxes**: an opaque front red box at XY `[-0.25,1.25]` and z `[0,0.5]`, and a smaller rear green box at XY `[0,1]` and z `[-0.5,-0.1]`. The rear box is serialized last and selected on import. The fixture is `.artifacts/contour-pickable-c129/independent-closed-nested-boxes.sgl`, 1,052 bytes, SHA-256 `91a2887cfea1b47bee0833ab83446bd842cfaf39441176e2ed578c5257a46838`; its independent writer and complete geometry manifest are retained beside it.

The first physical source attempt sampled outside the projected box and failed. The pinned SculptGL camera projected the front box to just 26×26 screen pixels in that attempt, although the final rendered object was much larger after its deferred fit. A corrected run scans the actual projected bounds, confirms a pick of source object 0, and sends a **physical Shift-click** through the normal browser input route. SculptGL's Brush lock-position setting prevents a dab; the before/after complete vertex arrays are identical, while selected indices change from `[1]` to `[1,0]`. OBrush adds the front part through its normal scene-panel Ctrl-click, changing selected IDs from `[2]` to `[2,1]`. The corresponding browser results are `.artifacts/contour-pickable-c129/source-ui-1792283224474/result.json` and `.artifacts/contour-pickable-c129/root-1792283147824/startup.json`. Failed source attempts remain in the same ignored artifact directory.

At matched 1140×825 viewport crops, both contour off/on toggles change **5,149 RGB pixels** on the selected union's outer silhouette. The pinned source's changed-pixel box is `[258,100,882,725]`, while OBrush's is `[258,101,882,724]` (exclusive upper bounds). Binary mask IoU is `0.6042997352`; all source changed pixels are within one pixel of OBrush's and 5,136/5,149 OBrush changed pixels are within one pixel of the source's. Every changed pixel lies within **two pixels** of the other mask. The source harness suppresses only the transient Brush cursor's `Selection.render` after the physical click so its independently positioned ring does not contaminate the contour diff; both ordinary contour renderers remain active. The measurements are display-space responses, not an exact RGBA or antialiasing match.

OBRush's normal contour checkbox off/on/off preserves the exact 5,384-byte saved project SHA-256 `66d72872481cff1c74ec8bb3436fb3dd976f7f43806a5c2331de1649a853cbec`; its off and after-off viewport crops are RGB-identical. Both successful browser runs have zero console errors, exceptions and blocked external requests. The OBrush app WASM is the unchanged frozen C126 build SHA-256 `5c78d1ce06ee2fcaf017d888c00c24299dcc634eb0779abcebb538134ec3918f`. [Machine-readable evidence](evidence/sculptgl-contour-physical-select-c129.json) records the fixture, hashes, browser runs, failed-attempt boundary and pixel measurements.

This closes a two-part **physical source selection and combined contour** comparison for this fixture. It does not prove three-part membership toggles, selection scope through duplicate/delete/isolate/transform/export, arbitrary curved/transparent/intersecting meshes, exact color/alpha samples, physical high-DPI/native GUI, sustained performance or complete SculptGL parity.
