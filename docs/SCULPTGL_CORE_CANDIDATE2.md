## Normal OBJ return

Open and Append now use the shared bounded `Mesh::import_obj` consumer. It retains suitable original quad faces, their canonical 0–2 render diagonal, and complete indexed corner UVs. Distinct equal-valued UV IDs and unused coordinate prefixes survive. Positive and negative vertex, texture and normal references use declarations already encountered. Normal declarations and references are validated; geometric vertex normals are derived rather than promising retention of arbitrary supplied normals.

Partial UV channels and malformed declarations reject before document replacement. Input, channel storage, scalar work and polygon triangulation have explicit limits. Concave quads with an unsuitable canonical diagonal and larger polygons become triangles with mapped corner UVs and a warning. This differs from SculptGL's quadrangulation policy. Object boundaries currently combine into one mesh; groups and MTL fields are reported gaps. Validated SculptGL `#MAT` bytes import roughness/metallic and convert unmasked weight to protection as `1-z`; exact raw unmasked-weight behavior remains a separate precision gap. The `#MRGB` leading mask byte is validated without interpreting its semantics.

## Material paint and subdivision

Paint exposes independent RGB, roughness and metallic write controls and normalized material targets. Defaults preserve OBrush's previous RGB-only behavior and implicit material `[0.18,0.08]`; SculptGL's defaults and response are different. A real material edit allocates channel storage lazily. Disabled channels, zero contact and other no-ops retain absence and redo. Material-only ordinary and locked strokes do not force vertex RGB display. Channel changes commit after the old stroke's endpoint. An explicit document transaction permits first material painting on old implicit retained levels without weakening generic attribute-installation guards.

Static smooth and linear-edge choices use distinct retained scheme metadata. They operate on original triangles/quads, preserve corner seams and channels, and exclude render diagonals from their edge graph. Existing Loop mathematics and project behavior remain separate. Independent rational and staged-f32 fixtures exercise the pinned Warren/mixed/quad stencils, boundary quirks, odd ordering and copied even channel values. Fine detail transport still uses OBrush world offsets; source tangent-frame analysis/synthesis, structural Reverse and stack-side deletion remain separate gates.
